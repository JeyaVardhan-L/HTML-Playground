Understanding the Shift to Test-Time Reasoning

Recent progress in artificial intelligence is shifting from raw parameter scale during pre-training to test-time compute. Instead of answering queries instantaneously, newer models are trained to pause and generate intermediate reasoning steps—verifying proofs, testing logic, and correcting missteps before producing a final response.
