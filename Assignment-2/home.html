Core Paradigm Shift

The Two Dimensions of Compute: Pre-Training vs. Test-Time Reasoning

Published for WAF Lab Assignment 2 · Technical Briefing

Historically, artificial intelligence progress was governed by pre-training scaling laws: feeding ever-larger clusters of graphics processors with trillions of multimodal tokens. While pre-training continues to expand baseline knowledge, frontier laboratories like OpenAI (o1, o3) and DeepSeek (R1) have unlocked an entirely new scaling axis: Test-Time Compute.

By allowing a neural model to generate thousands of private intermediate reasoning tokens—forming explicit hypothesis trees, verifying proofs, and correcting intermediate miscalculations—frontier systems achieve PhD-level competitive performance in physics, competitive mathematics, and complex software engineering.
